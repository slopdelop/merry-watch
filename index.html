About the collection

New Christmas films and standalone specials for 2026 from Hallmark+, Netflix, Prime Video, Apple TV and Tubi, plus selected festive competitions. Hallmark films stream the day after their TV premieres; other services use their own announced dates. The original listing is linked in every title.

Scripted series such as Mistletoe Murders are excluded. Standalone holiday specials and selected festive baking or competition shows are included. Individual scripted-series episodes are excluded.

Catalogue checked October 24, 2026. This V1 catalogue is curated; it does not refresh itself automatically. US availability.
